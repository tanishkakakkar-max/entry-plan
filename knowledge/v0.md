# v0 definition

## The sentence
A brand enters its product, brand identity, target customer and one of four countries (Japan, Singapore, India, US), and gets back a staked entry plan: a recommended entry point, a phased entry strategy, consumer psychology insights for that market, and the risks and rewards of going there - grounded in Tanishka's country research, not generic AI knowledge.

## Inputs (the form)
- Brand name
- What you sell (product + category)
- Brand identity (positioning, values, voice, in their words)
- Target customer (who buys today / who you want)
- Price point
- Where you sell today + stage (pre-launch, selling domestically, already international)
- Target country: Japan | Singapore | India | US

## Output (the plan) - same 9 sections every time
1. The call (one-paragraph bottom line + go / test first / not yet)
2. Recommended entry point (city, channel, customer segment)
3. Entry strategy (phased: first 90 days, months 3-9, months 9-18)
4. Consumer psychology (what this market's buyer believes, and what the brand must change because of it)
5. Brand adaptation (keep / change / drop)
6. Competitive reality (who already owns the space)
7. Risks (regulatory, tariff/cost, cultural fit, operational) with mitigation
8. Rewards (what success unlocks)
9. Validate before you spend (the 3-5 cheapest tests) + confidence level

## Rules the tool must follow
- Every fact turns into a decision ("so what"). No PEST data dumps.
- Take a position. One recommended route, not a menu of five.
- Use the country research doc first; flag anything that comes from general knowledge.
- Never invent statistics. If a number isn't in the research, say what to check instead.
- Say when the honest answer is "not yet" or "wrong market."

## Out of scope for v0
- Countries beyond the four
- Accounts, payments, saving plans (free tool, no login)
- Live web research inside the tool
- The paid tier (custom plans by Tanishka)

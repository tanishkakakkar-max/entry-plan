# CAVA brand teardown (facts checked October 2026)

## What CAVA is
- Fast-casual Mediterranean: build-your-own bowls, pitas, salads; signature dips (Crazy Feta, hummus); digital ordering and pickup; a grocery CPG line of dips, spreads and dressings. Founded 2010 in Washington DC as the fast-casual spinoff of a full-service Mediterranean restaurant (Cava Mezze, 2006). Co-founder and CEO Brett Schulman.
- NYSE: CAVA, IPO June 2023 at $22 a share, raised about $318M (Nation's Restaurant News). Acquired Zoës Kitchen in 2018 and converted units.
- All restaurants company-owned and US-only. The FY2025 10-K mentions no international plans or franchising; the only "license agreements" reference is in revenue definitions. Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1639438/000162828026011296/cava-20251228.htm

## Scale and performance
- Fiscal 2025 year end (Dec 28, 2025): 439 restaurants in 28 states and DC; digital revenue mix 37.9% (36.4% in 2024); restaurants 2,000-3,000 sq ft, 30-60 indoor seats; loyalty program launched nationwide 2024, "Sea, Sand, Sun" tiers added 2025. (10-K above.)
- Q1 2026 (ended Apr 19, 2026): revenue $434.4M, up 32.2%; same-restaurant sales up 9.7% (traffic 6.8%); 20 net new; 459 restaurants; restaurant-level margin 25.1%; 2026 guidance 75-77 net new openings. Source: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001639438/000162828026036461/earningsrelease2026q1.htm
- Q2 2026: revenue $365.4M, up 31.3%; same-restaurant sales up 9.0% (traffic 5.3%); 17 net new; 476 restaurants; restaurant-level margin 25.7%. Source: https://www.investing.com/news/earnings/cava-group-surges-on-revenue-beat-robust-traffic-growth-4852682
- 500th restaurant opened Sep 23, 2026 in Minneapolis (first Minnesota store); now 30 states plus DC; company says it will surpass 1,000 US restaurants by 2032. Source: https://www.fastcasual.com/news/cava-reaches-500-restaurant-milestone-with-minneapolis-opening/
- Price discipline: kept menu price increases below inflation while Sweetgreen's comps fell nearly 13% in Q1 2026 (NRN, May 19, 2026): https://www.nrn.com/fast-casual/cava-raises-guidance-after-a-strong-1q
- CFO on tariffs (May 2025): most of the supply chain is domestic. Source: https://schwabnetwork.com/video/cava-cfo-on-expansion-plans-incredible-value-proposition
- CEO said a seafood item is coming (Bloomberg video, Feb 25, 2026): https://www.bloomberg.com/news/videos/2026-02-25/cava-ceo-going-to-be-launching-seafood-item-video

## Expansion playbook
- "Coastal smile" strategy at IPO: dense East Coast base, then Sun Belt, then West Coast (NRN, 2023). Midwest entry via Chicago (Wicker Park, Apr 2024; 325 stores at the time): https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240424050498/en/cava-enters-the-midwest-with-its-first-chicago-restaurant/
- 2026 new markets: Cincinnati, St. Louis, Columbus (Q1), Minneapolis (Sep). Pattern: enter a new metro with more than one signed site; build density; share supply hubs.

## Lessons from peers (US)
- Sweetgreen: 281 units end-2025; comps fell every quarter of 2025 (-11.5% Q4, about -8% FY); 2026 guide -2% to -4%; only 15 openings planned for 2026; CEO admitted pricing was seen as too high; adding $10.95 wraps and $10 loyalty options; closing leases as they expire. Source: https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/operations/sweetgreen-wanted-reach-1000-units-end-decade-it-may-take-bit-longer
- Chipotle (closest scale analog, 3,700+ units, 90+ international incl. 58 Canada, 20 UK, 6 France, 2 Germany) goes abroad through development partners: Alshaya Group in the Middle East (Jul 2023, first opening 2024) and Alsea in Mexico (signed Apr 21, 2025, first opening early 2026): https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/chipotle-alsea-development-agreement-mexico-2026/745855/
- Guzman y Gomez (Australian build-your-own fast casual) exited the US in May 2026 after its Chicago restaurants missed targets; one-off hit US$30-40M. Lesson for any foreign fast casual in the US: sub-scale tests die. Source: https://www.marketing-interactive.com/guzman-y-gomez-exits-us-market-to-refocus-on-australia-singapore-and-japan

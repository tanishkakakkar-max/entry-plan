# Ceramics and handmade home goods: category notes (checked October 2026)

## Peer-reviewed anchor
- Fuchs, Schreier & van Osselaer (2015), "The Handmade Effect: What's Love Got to Do with It?", Journal of Marketing 79(2), 98-110. Consumers see handmade products as containing the maker's love and find them more attractive; the effect is strongest for gifts to close relationships and weakens for distant ones. So what: gifting (weddings, housewarming, seasonal gifts) is the sharpest occasion for any handmade brand. Summary: https://www.monash.edu/business/acrs/retail-trends/show-all/sustainable-choices/made-with-love

## Japan
- Tableware is a food-contact product under the Food Sanitation Act. Importers file a food import notification with the MHLW quarantine station with ingredient and process documents; ceramics covered by the apparatus standards need a test report from an MHLW-registered lab or an official exporting-country report (lead, cadmium). HS codes: porcelain 6911.10, ceramic 6912.00. Labeling under the Household Goods Quality Labeling Act applies to plastic, glass and lacquer tableware. JETRO (updated Sep 2025): https://www.jetro.go.jp/world/qa/M-010810.html
- EU-origin goods: the Japan-EU EPA sets preferential rates; the kanzei tariff schedule shows EU-origin cotton knitwear at "Free"; check 6911 and 6912 line by line [verify]. https://www.kanzei.or.jp/statistical/tariff/detail/index/e/610910010
- Domestic benchmark: Hasami ware (Nagasaki), about 400 years old, made for ordinary people, repositioned since 2002 as affordable everyday tableware. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasami_ware
- EU-Japan Centre webinar on ceramic tableware: demand drivers are an aging population seeking healthy living, younger consumers open to European lifestyle, and more single-person households. https://www.eu-japan.eu/publications/ceramic-tableware-market-japan-minerva-felowship
- Prime retail rents (Savills, Jun 2026): Shibuya 1F ¥45,500 per tsubo; Ginza ¥63,700. https://realestateasia.com/commercial-retail/in-focus/tokyo-retail-rents-rise-vacancy-remains-near-zero-in-prime-corridors

## Singapore
- Local tableware scene and prices (City Nomads, May 2023): Costa Nova (Portugal) at House of AnLi from S$25-38; Padme Hum studio plates from S$100 for two; Suterwala's from S$15; Table Matters 20-piece set S$135; Japanese ceramics via KURIĒTO. https://citynomads.com/where-to-buy-tableware-dinnerware-in-singapore-pretty-plates-cups-and-more/
- Cautionary case: Naiise (founded 2013, the biggest platform for local designers) sold on consignment with 30-40% commission, fell behind on vendor payments from 2016, lost brands in 2020, closed its last store (Jewel Changi) in April 2021. Lesson: firm wholesale orders, never consignment with an undercapitalized retailer. https://vulcanpost.com/741380/naiise-closes-jewel-changi-airport-outlet-singapore
- Prime retail rents (Knight Frank, 2Q2026): Orchard S$31.90 psf per month. https://www.edgeprop.sg/property-news/stable-retail-property-rents-2q2026-despite-ongoing-churn-knight-frank-singapore
- Duty-free for most goods; GST 9%; overseas-vendor GST registration thresholds for low-value goods [verify with IRAS].

## India
- Premium Indian tableware prices (Outlook Business): Nicobar stoneware Rs 500-6,800 a piece; Good Earth full sets about Rs 2 lakh; Casa Paradox about Rs 2,000 a piece. https://www.outlookbusiness.com/cest-la-vie-6/the-good-life-24/serve-it-right-3793
- Import duty on HS 6912 ceramic tableware: basic customs duty plus social welfare surcharge plus IGST; pull current rates from CBIC [verify]. https://www.seair.co.in/custom-duty/ceramic-tableware-kitchenware-household-articles-sub-heading-6912.aspx
- Gifting: Technopak put the Indian gifting industry at about Rs 2.5 lakh crore; personalization and social media drive it (Outlook Business, 2021/2023). https://www.outlookbusiness.com/invest/gifting-sector-makes-steady-growth-in-upscale-india-7521

## United States
- Portugal exported about US$100.7M of ceramic products to the US in 2025 (UN COMTRADE via Trading Economics). https://tradingeconomics.com/portugal/exports/united-states/ceramic-products
- US-EU framework (Aug 2025): tariffs on most EU goods capped at 15%; confirm application to HS 6912 and stacking with a broker [verify]. https://www.semafor.com/article/08/21/2025/us-agrees-to-cap-tariffs-on-europe-at-15-in-trade-deal-framework
- FDA limits on leachable lead and cadmium in ceramic ware; California bans sales above those limits, requires Prop 65 warnings above safe-harbor levels and a permanent maker's or importer's mark on tableware containing lead or cadmium; no pre-import notification for tableware. JETRO (updated Jan 2026): https://www.jetro.go.jp/world/qa/04A-041131.html
- Etsy FY2025: 86.5M active buyers, 5.6M active sellers, GMS $10.46B; 74% of GMS from US buyers, 26% outside the US. https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1370637/000137063726000019/etsy-20251231.htm
- Faire: 100K+ brands, 700K independent retailers; retailers get net-60 and can return unsold first-order goods; brands paid up front; 15% take rate on standard orders, 0% on a new retailer's first order via Faire Direct (Contrary Research, Mar 2024). https://research.contrary.com/company/faire
- East Fork (Asheville, 2009): own factory, limited color drops that sell out; reviewers call non-core items "pretty steep." https://thingtesting.com/brands/east-fork

# Apparel category notes for India-made natural-fiber brands (checked October 2026)

## Peer-reviewed anchors
- Acikgoz, Busalim, Lynn & Iahad, "Understanding Gen Z consumers' perceptions of sustainable fashion clothing: A PLS-SEM and fsQCA approach," Journal of Business Research 208 (2026), doi 10.1016/j.jbusres.2026.116048. Environmental concern is the strongest driver of purchase intention (β 0.76); knowledge helps (β 0.12); image barriers including greenwashing suspicion reduce adoption; price, social risk and tradition barriers not significant in the main model but matter in some configurations; authors call for credible eco-labels and transparent communication, and note affordability and convenience can override values. So what: prove, don't preach; certificates and factory on the page. https://doras.dcu.ie/32854/2/Understanding%20Gen%20Z%20consumers%E2%80%99%20perceptions%20of%20sustainable%20fashion%20clothing.pdf
- Tran et al. (2022), "Eco-friendly fashion among generation Z," PLoS ONE 17(8) e0272789, 313 Vietnamese Gen Z: perceived behavioral control drives intention; product-service quality is the only factor affecting loyalty; knowledge and attitudes alone did not persuade young customers to pay; businesses should prioritize quality over green marketing. https://doaj.org/article/35e8904a525a4564ba655cf14267ae41

## Japan
- Tariff: Japan's schedule for HS 6109.10.010 (cotton knitted T-shirts) shows general rate 10.9%, and "Free" for India under the Japan-India EPA (also Free for EU, ASEAN, CPTPP; China under RCEP 6.8%). Page dated 2026-08-08. Check other apparel lines (6204, 6104, 6110) and the certificate-of-origin process [verify]. https://www.kanzei.or.jp/statistical/tariff/detail/index/e/610910010
- ifs Future Laboratory and Re-Creation survey (Dec 2025, n=1,160, greater Tokyo, ages 20-38): about half of fashion-conscious digital natives wear low-impact clothing because they find it stylish; over half would accept a price increase of 3% or more and nearly 20% 10% or more; main barrier is being unable to judge whether an item is genuine, then distrust of corporate claims and limited range; over 80% want to keep clothes as long as possible (mottainai); TV still the top information source. https://zenbird.media/?p=30636
- BCG Japan sustainable consumer survey (Jan 2026, n=3,300): 35% would pay a 10%+ premium for CO2-reduced coffee, 41% after explanation; Gen Z: 52% bought a used or refurbished item in the past year, 31% factor resale value into purchases. https://www.bcg.com/ja-jp/press/1june2026-sustainable-consumer-survey
- Competitors: MUJI organic cotton basics (country doc); gelato pique (Mash Style Lab) roomwear, now exporting via a Sep 2026 LUMINE Singapore pop-up with a climate-edited summer range. https://littlebigreddot.com/?p=23743
- Labeling: Household Goods Quality Labeling Act, Japanese fiber composition and care labels, JIS care symbols [verify].

## United States
- Tariffs (Jul 24, 2026): Section 301 surcharge of 10% on Indian goods (lower tier; China, Vietnam, Thailand at 12.5%), stacking on normal MFN duty; India did not receive the textile and apparel tariff-rate-quota exemption; Indian textile exports to the US about $11B a year. Cotton knit tops MFN 16.5% per the country doc [verify both with a broker]. https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/heres-breaking-down-the-section-301-tariffs-545110-2026-07-24
- De minimis suspended since Aug 29, 2025 (country doc) [verify].
- Organic claims: USDA NOP Policy Memo 11-14 (2011): a textile may be labeled organic only if every fiber labeled organic was produced and certified under NOP; GOTS-certified textiles may be sold as organic but may not use the USDA seal or imply NOP certification; no mixing of organic and non-organic sources for a fiber labeled organic. https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/documents/public_events/975753/nop-pm-11-14-labelingoftextiles.pdf

# Beauty regulation and channels by country (checked October 2026)

## Japan
- PMD Act: cosmetics vs quasi-drugs (quasi-drugs need approved actives and face a stricter regime). Importers need a cosmetic marketing licence; marketing notification to the prefecture before import; foreign manufacturer notification to PMDA; Japanese labels with company, product name, batch, shelf life, full ingredient list, origin and an irritation warning; efficacy claims only from the approved list; licences valid 5 years. Applications typically filed by a Japanese marketing authorization holder. https://cosmetic.chemlinked.com/cosmepedia/japan-cosmetic-regulation
- Hydrocolloid acne patches: most sold as general goods; some registered as Class I general medical devices. Classification depends on claims and construction [verify with a Japanese regulatory consultant]. https://onecosme.jp/92126
- Channels: Korean beauty is surging (Korean lipstick 55.0% of Japan's lipstick imports in 2025, up 19.7%); stocked at Loft and Matsumoto Kiyoshi; Qoo10 Japan strong for Korean skincare. Rakuten and Amazon.co.jp for general-goods patches. https://en.edaily.co.kr/news/eda202607025565/

## Singapore
- HSA regulates cosmetics under the ASEAN Cosmetic Directive (since 2008). Notify HSA before sale; supply only after acknowledgement; renew yearly; one notification per variant; fees S$13 (S$28 for eye, lip, oral and some hair dye products) for the first three variants, S$8 each after; labels in English with product name and function, directions, full ingredients, origin, responsible company, contents, batch, dates, precautions. Cosmetics may not claim to treat or prevent disease; products intended for treatment (e.g. acne treatment creams) are not cosmetics; check HSA's classification guide for borderline products. https://cosmetic.chemlinked.com/cosmepedia/singapore-cosmetic-regulation
- Channels: Watsons (carries Hero and Garnier patches), Shopee, TikTok Shop (launched 2022 with commission waived, 1% payment fee at launch; current fees [verify]). https://www.marketing-interactive.com/tiktok-shops-sg
- GST 9%; most cosmetics enter duty-free [verify].

## India
- Imported cosmetics must be registered with CDSCO under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act 1940 and the Cosmetics Rules 2020 through an Indian importer or authorized agent; Legal Metrology labeling and MRP apply. Fees and timelines [verify]. https://www.freyrsolutions.com/blog/cosmetic-product-registration-in-india-a-step-by-step-cdsco-guide
- Channels: Nykaa (beauty platform; owns Dot & Key); Amazon.in; quick commerce is reshaping beauty: beauty and personal care is 13.4% of Blinkit's daily sales; Zepto's beauty business grew fivefold in a year with average beauty order about Rs 550; NielsenIQ: e-commerce and quick-commerce beauty sales up 39% (Jun-Nov 2024) vs 3% in stores; premium brands struggle with the lack of storytelling space. https://www.theestablished.com/self/beauty/the-rise-and-rise-of-beauty-on-indias-quick-commerce-platforms
- Nykaa Fashion FY25 GMV Rs 3,800 crore (+12%); Myntra and Flipkart Fashion about half of e-commerce apparel; fashion returns 20-25%. https://inc42.com/features/nykaa-fashion-running-out-of-vogue/
- TikTok is banned; Instagram Reels, YouTube and WhatsApp carry discovery and service.

## United States
- MoCRA: manufacturers register facilities (renew every two years) and a responsible person lists each cosmetic product (update yearly); small-business exemptions; products regulated as drugs or devices are exempt from the cosmetic rules. https://www.fda.gov/cosmetics/cosmetics-news-events/fda-provides-summary-data-registration-and-listing-cosmetic-product-facilities-and-products
- TikTok Shop US beauty GMV $980M in Q2 2026 (+82% YoY); skincare the largest category at $844.1M over 12 months (+123%); Medicube led Q2 at $62.8M (Charm.io via Beauty Independent, Jul 14, 2026). https://www.beautyindependent.com/tiktok-shop-nears-1b-beauty-sales-second-straight-quarter/

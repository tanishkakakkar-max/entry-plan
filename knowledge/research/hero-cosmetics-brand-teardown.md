# Hero Cosmetics teardown (facts checked October 2026)

## Ownership and scale
- Founded 2017 by Ju Rhyu with Dwight Lee and Andrew Lee (about $50,000 pooled); idea came from Korean hydrocolloid patches while Rhyu worked for Samsung in Korea. Launched on Amazon; the $12.99 price unlocked Prime eligibility and the first batch sold out in 90 days. Over 1 billion patches sold; Alix Earle named first global face. Fortune, Jul 8, 2024: https://fortune.com/2024/07/08/k-beauty-mighty-patch-pimple-hydrocolloid-hero-cosmetics-ju-rhyu-amazon-prime
- Acquired by Church & Dwight for $630M, announced Sep 6, 2022. At the time: about $1.5M revenue expected in 2019, $100M in 2021, $140M expected in 2022; No. 2 US acne brand with 14% share behind Neutrogena; 63% of the patch subcategory; Amazon review count five times the next competitor; patches had grown to 18% of the US acne category; Hero's distribution only 8% ACV vs 99% for acne treatments, with a stated goal of 80-90%. Glossy: https://www.glossy.co/beauty/beauty-wellness-briefing-with-church-dwights-acquisition-of-hero-cosmetics-whats-next-for-the-pimple-patch-space
- Church & Dwight Q3 2025: HERO named among domestic growth leaders and, with THERABREATH and BATISTE, among international growth leaders. https://businesswire.com/news/home/20251031905732/en/Church-Dwight-Reports-Q3-2025-Results

## Evidence on hydrocolloid for acne
- Chao et al. (2006), Journal of Cosmetic Science 57(2): randomized double-blind pilot, 20 patients; the hydrocolloid "Acne Dressing" group showed significantly greater reduction in severity, redness, oiliness and sebum over 3-7 days than skin tape; UVB transmission 7.4% vs 38%. Very small sample; authors call for a larger study. So what: "science-backed" should be stated with limits; a larger study would be a competitive asset. https://library.scconline.org/v057n02/1

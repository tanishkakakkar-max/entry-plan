# Food delivery platforms by country (checked October 2026)

## Peer-reviewed baseline
- Li & Wang (2024), "On-Demand Delivery Platforms and Restaurant Sales," Management Science (open access). Platforms raise total takeout sales and spill over into dine-in. They complement chain restaurants' own takeout but cannibalize independents' takeout; the overall positive effect for fast-food chains is about four times that for independents. Platforms "facilitate price competition" and erode independents' ability to differentiate. So what: a chain should treat delivery as incremental volume; an independent should expect substitution. https://udspace.udel.edu/items/7573ae2a-f402-4799-9955-918115f91993

## Japan
- Market led by Uber Eats Japan and Demae-can. Wolt withdrew in March 2026 after about six years. Demae-can expected a net loss of ¥4B for the year to August 2026, its eighth straight loss. Domestic market ¥860.3B in 2023 (Circana Japan), down 6.1% in 2024, up 2.0% in 2025. https://www.thestar.com.my/aseanplus/aseanplus-news/2026/07/04/japan-food-delivery-firms-at-crossroads-as-inflation-squeezes-profits
- Uber Eats Japan introduced a store-price-parity scheme in March 2026; about 22,000 of its 120,000+ member restaurants joined and reported sales up about 80% on average; costs borne by the platform and users. Delivery penetration in Japan is lower than the US and Korea. https://www.nippon.com/en/news/yjj2026080900177/
- Commission rates: no reliable public figure found; ask the platforms directly [verify].

## Singapore
- After Deliveroo's exit (Mar 4, 2026), Grab and foodpanda are the two major platforms. Analysts expect higher fees and fewer promotions over time. https://www.ntu.edu.sg/business/news-events/news/story-detail/deliveroo%27s-singapore-exit-may-lead-to-higher-fees--fewer-discounts--analysts-say
- Shares: Grab about 69%, foodpanda 24%, Deliveroo about 7% before exit (TechNode Global). https://technode.global/?p=992113
- Grab stated merchant commissions of 25-30% of order value in April 2020 (worked example: restaurant keeps 70% of an S$18 order). Current rate [verify]. https://vulcanpost.com/696332/grab-responds-and-provides-actual-breakdown-of-merchant-commission
- Blackbox Research (Jul 2026, n=1,001): 64% of households use food delivery; 92% of active users default to GrabFood or foodpanda; 57% feel choice has shrunk. https://asianews.network/?p=301312

## India
- Zomato (parent Eternal) and Swiggy dominate; new entrants Rapido's Ownly (live in Bengaluru) and magicpin; Flipkart exploring entry. Both leaders shifting investment to quick commerce (Blinkit, Instamart). https://www.business-standard.com/amp/topic/food-delivery-in-india
- Commission rates: contested between platforms and NRAI; no single reliable figure found [verify with NRAI or the platforms].

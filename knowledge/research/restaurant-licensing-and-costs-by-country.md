# Restaurant licensing, ingredient import and cost notes (checked October 2026)

## Japan
- Food business licence (営業許可) from the local public health centre; a food hygiene manager must be appointed; fees vary by city (Yokohama lists ¥16,000-18,000 for a restaurant business licence). https://en.city.yokohama.lg.jp/kohoku/kenko-iryo-fukushi/kenko_iryo/shoku/kaisetu-s.html
- Imported food and ingredients: the importer must file a food import notification with the MHLW quarantine station for every shipment; goods cannot be sold without the Certificate of Notification; meat products need an exporting-country sanitary certificate; quarantine stations offer pre-import consultation. https://www.mhlw.go.jp/english/topics/importedfoods/1.html
- Leases: a company lease may be declined or need a guarantor unless a resident with appropriate status sits on the board; restaurants have a higher closure rate than other businesses. https://www.rsm.global/japan/shiodome/en/node/201
- Prime retail rents (Savills via Real Estate Asia, Jun 2026): 1F asking rents per tsubo, Ginza ¥63,700, Omotesando ¥54,100, Shinjuku ¥53,500, Shibuya ¥45,500; prime-street vacancy near zero. https://realestateasia.com/commercial-retail/in-focus/tokyo-retail-rents-rise-vacancy-remains-near-zero-in-prime-corridors

## Singapore
- SFA Food Shop Licence, S$195 a year, applied through GoBusiness; applicants must be Singapore citizens, PRs, registered societies or ACRA-registered entities; tenancy agreement needed only at the final stage; food handlers need hygiene certificates. https://www.gobusiness.gov.sg/browse-all-licences/Singapore-Food-Agency-(SFA)/Food-Shop-Licence and https://www.sfa.gov.sg/food-retail/licence-permit
- MUIS halal certification (Eating Establishment scheme): at least 2 Muslim staff per premises (3 for catering or central kitchen); Halal Team trained (Level 1 Halal Foundation Programme); only declared halal ingredients; no pork contact on equipment; application fees before GST: restaurant up to 186 sqm S$775, above S$1,140; normal processing under 14 working days; renewal yearly. https://dollarsandsense.sg/business/how-apply-halal-certification-fb-singapore/ (scheme page: https://www.muis.gov.sg/halal/for-business/scheme-types---eligibility-criteria/)
- Prime retail rents (Knight Frank, 2Q2026): Orchard S$31.90 psf per month; island-wide average S$29; Marina Centre-City Hall-Bugis S$28.10; recent closures mostly F&B. https://www.edgeprop.sg/property-news/stable-retail-property-rents-2q2026-despite-ongoing-churn-knight-frank-singapore

## India
- FDI: up to 100% in QSRs under the automatic route (restaurants and cloud kitchens). https://www.mondaq.com/india/inward-foreign-investment/1535116/fdi-in-restaurants-a-gateway-to-enhanced-customer-experience
- FSSAI: Basic Registration (turnover under Rs 12 lakh), State Licence (up to Rs 20 crore), Central Licence (above Rs 20 crore, or any multi-state operation or import/export regardless of turnover). One licence per premises. Thresholds [verify current]. https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/sih/en/bloglist/blogs/all_about_food_licensing.html
- Other licences typically needed: Shops and Establishment registration, Eating House licence (police), health/trade licence, fire NOC, GST, music licence (PPL) [verify per state].

## United States
- Restaurants need state and city health permits plus FDA labeling and allergen rules for packaged items [verify by jurisdiction]. Tariffs on imported ingredients changed repeatedly in 2025-2026; re-verify quarterly with a customs broker.

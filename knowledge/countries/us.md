## 3. Consumer psychology (each insight -> so what)
- Convenience expectations are set by Amazon: fast shipping, free returns, easy checkout. -> Slow cross-border shipping is a conversion killer; plan US fulfillment early.
- Founder story and brand personality matter; shoppers buy into people and communities. -> Founder-led content is a real channel, not a nice-to-have.
- Reviews and social proof are decisive, especially on TikTok and Amazon. -> Seed reviews and creator content before scaling paid ads.
- Ethical and wellness claims are common and shoppers are skeptical of vague ones. -> Specific, provable claims (fiber %, certification name, factory) beat broad ones.
- Huge regional variation in climate, taste and price tolerance. -> Pick a beachhead region and audience.

## 4. Entry routes (when each one fits)
| Route | Fits when | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| DTC (Shopify) + US 3PL | Strong brand and content | Paid acquisition costs are high |
| Amazon | Searchable product, review-driven category | Price pressure, brand control |
| TikTok Shop | Visual, demo-able product, creator-friendly | Fast cycles, heavy creator commissions |
| Wholesale marketplaces (e.g. Faire) to independent boutiques | Design-led product, small batch | Wholesale margins |
| Big-box / department retail (Target, Nordstrom, Ulta, Sephora) | Proven demand, ability to fund inventory and marketing | Chargebacks, slotting, volume demands |
| Distributor / importer | Food, beverage, regulated goods | Margin stacking (three-tier system for alcohol) |

## 5. Regulations, tariffs, costs (fast-moving - re-verify each quarter)
- Tariffs: the regime changed repeatedly in 2025-2026. As of an Oct 2026 check of secondary sources: the Supreme Court struck down IEEPA tariffs (Feb 20, 2026); a 10% Section 122 surcharge ran Feb-Jul 2026; since Jul 24, 2026 a tiered Section 301 tariff (10% or 12.5% by country) applies on top of normal MFN duty, with India in the 10% tier and under legal challenge [VERIFY with CBP / a customs broker]. Example: cotton knit tops (HTS 6109.10.00) carry 16.5% MFN duty plus the 10% surcharge [VERIFY].
- De minimis: the $800 duty-free exemption for low-value shipments was suspended for all countries from Aug 29, 2025 [VERIFY current status]. -> Shipping single orders cross-border into the US now carries duties and brokerage; bulk import to a US warehouse is usually cheaper.
- (Apparel) Textile labeling: Textile Fiber Products Identification Act - fiber content, country of origin, manufacturer identity; care labeling rule [VERIFY].
- Environmental claims: FTC Green Guides govern claims like "eco-friendly" or "organic"; vague claims are risky [VERIFY latest update status].
- (Beauty) FDA MoCRA - facility registration and product listing [VERIFY]; California has additional ingredient laws; Prop 65 warnings in California.
- (Food, Restaurants) FDA registration, labeling, allergen rules; restaurants also need state and city health permits [VERIFY].
- Sales tax: varies by state; economic nexus thresholds after Wayfair [VERIFY].

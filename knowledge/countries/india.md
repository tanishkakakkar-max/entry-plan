## 0. Thesis (the non-consensus point of view)
[TANI] Draft to react to: "India" is not one market. A foreign brand that enters "India" usually means it enters metro, English-speaking, Instagram-native shoppers in a few cities - a market of real size, but one that behaves very differently from the rest of the country. The winning move is to pick that slice deliberately and price for it, not to chase national scale.

## 3. Consumer psychology (each insight -> so what)
- Price-value scrutiny is intense, even among affluent shoppers; discounts and sale events (e.g. festive season around Diwali) drive a large share of buying [VERIFY]. -> Plan launch pricing with festive promotions in mind; don't launch premium into the middle of a discount season without a reason.
- Trust signals: cash on delivery, easy returns, reviews, and recognizable marketplaces lower first-purchase risk [VERIFY current COD share]. -> Offer COD or marketplace presence early; DTC-only with prepaid can stall.
- Foreign brands carry aspiration, but local D2C brands have raised the bar on price and relevance. -> "Imported" is not enough; the brand needs India-specific relevance.
- Family and community influence purchase decisions more than in the US. -> Content and offers that work for gifting and family can widen the funnel.
- Regional and language diversity is huge. -> Start with one or two metros and English plus Hindi; expand language only with demand.
- (Apparel) Climate: hot most of the year in much of the country. -> Breathable natural fabrics have a functional, not just ethical, story.

## 5. Regulations, tariffs, costs
### Import duties, taxes and entry rules (checked October 2026)
From the Oct 15, 2026 import-duty research (official customs and regulator sources where available; third-party lookups labeled).

#### How landed cost is built
1. **Basic Customs Duty (BCD)** by 8-digit tariff item, any exemption notification, and preferential origin.
2. **Social Welfare Surcharge (SWS)**, commonly 10% of BCD (exempt on some lines; Budget 2025 exempted 82 lines that carry a cess). [DHL India, Jun 2025](https://www.dhl.com/discover/en-in/logistics-advice/import-export-advice/import-duty-in-india), [India Briefing, Feb 2025](https://www.india-briefing.com/news/india-revises-customs-tariff-under-union-budget-2025-26-36054.html)
3. **AIDC, Health Cess, anti-dumping, safeguard or other levies** where applicable.
4. **IGST** at the domestic GST rate for the product, on value plus BCD and customs levies. [GST Council, imports](https://gstcouncil.gov.in/sites/default/files/e-version-gst-flyers/Imports_in_GST_Regime.pdf)

**Rule:** never give a single "India tariff" for a category. The plan must tell the brand to run the live [ICEGATE Customs Duty Calculator](https://www.icegate.gov.in/cdc) or [Trade Guide](https://www.icegate.gov.in/Webappl/Trade-Guide-on-Imports) with its 8-digit code, origin and date, because exemption notifications and FTA rates override the schedule.

- How the stack compounds (formula illustration, not a rate): BCD 20%, SWS 2%, IGST 18% → 100 × 1.22 × 1.18 = **about 44%** above value; with IGST 5%, about 28%.
- **So what:** on these indicative numbers, imported consumer goods can land roughly 28-51% above value before margin, which is why brands that scale in India manufacture locally or partner with an Indian manufacturer. Confirm the actual figure on ICEGATE.

#### Personal-use imports (heading 9804): not for inventory
- From **April 1, 2026**, BCD on dutiable goods imported for personal use is **10%** (previously 10% or 20%). [Budget speech 2026-27](https://www.indiabudget.gov.in/doc/budget_speech.pdf), [LKS](https://www.lkslaw.com/insights/news/union-budget-2026-changes-in-customs-law-and-duty-rates) Customs guidance applies SWS at 10% of BCD and IGST at 28% on 9804. [CBIC courier guidance](https://courier.cbic.gov.in/PN%20Bengaluru/PN%20issued%20by%20Bangalore%20Customs%20on%20duty%20structure.pdf) Illustration: ₹100,000 → BCD ₹10,000 + SWS ₹1,000 + IGST ₹31,080 = **about 42%**.
- Caution: the 28% GST slab was abolished in the Sept 2025 rationalization (except a 40% rate for specified goods), so the 28% IGST on 9804 may be outdated [VERIFY current IGST on 9804 with CBIC]. Never use 9804 rates for commercial stock, and don't treat the ₹10,000 gift and sample definitions in courier rules as an e-commerce de minimis.

#### Trade agreements now in force
- **India-EFTA TEPA** (Switzerland, Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein), in force Oct 1, 2025: Swiss watches (22%) go to zero within about five years; chocolate cut in Oct 2025 and Jan 2026, falling to zero; dairy, gold, generic drugs and cars excluded. [Swissinfo](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/swiss-trade/explainer-watches-win-pharma-waits-as-swiss-india-trade-deal-turns-one/92183947)
- **India-UK CETA**, in force July 15, 2026, with phased cuts (Scotch whisky, cars, machinery). [Chambers, 2026](https://chambers.com/articles/india-uk-ceta-in-force-what-it-means-for-businesses-2)
- Also agreements with Japan, Singapore/ASEAN and Australia. [Ministry of Commerce, trade agreements](https://www.commerce.gov.in/ministryofcommerce/node/4509) A preference applies only to eligible lines and originating goods with proof ([DGFT e-certificates of origin](https://coo.dgft.gov.in/)); "shipped from an FTA country" is not enough. No India-U.S. agreement covers consumer goods, so U.S.-made goods pay MFN rates.

#### Entry rules by category (non-tariff)
- (All, physical retail) **Single-brand retail FDI:** 100% foreign ownership on the automatic route since 2018. Above 51% foreign ownership, 30% of goods must be sourced in India (global sourcing from India counts for the first five years). Online sales are allowed, but a **physical store must open within two years**. [Treelife, Jul 2025](https://treelife.in/startups/cheat-sheet-for-fdi-in-single-brand-retail-trading) An Indian distributor or marketplace seller avoids these conditions at first.
- (Beauty) **CDSCO import registration** (Form COS-1 on SUGAM, certificate Form COS-2) by the manufacturer's authorized Indian agent or importer; US$1,000 per category, US$500 per site, US$50 per variant; about 90 days. [ChemLinked, 2021](https://cosmetic.chemlinked.com/news/cosmetic-news/india-clarifies-regulatory-requirements-for-cosmetic-import-registration) [VERIFY current fees]
- (Food, Health & wellness, Restaurants) IEC, a central **FSSAI importer licence**, and clearance through [FSSAI's FICS](https://fssai.gov.in/fics) for each consignment, with sampling and lab tests (about five days). Labels need the importer's name, FSSAI logo and licence number, veg/non-veg mark, best-before, ingredients and origin. **Remaining shelf life must exceed 60%** at clearance. [India Briefing, updated Feb 2025](https://india-briefing.com/news/food-import-procedure-india-fssai-steps-reforms-29980.html) Products presented as drugs, medical devices, AYUSH or therapeutic may fall outside food rules [VERIFY].
- (Technology) **BIS compulsory registration** covers laptops, notebooks and tablets, mobile phones, power adapters for IT equipment, power banks, and wireless headphones and earphones, among others. [BIS, Scheme II](https://www.bis.gov.in/product-certification/products-under-compulsory-certification/scheme-ii-registration-scheme) Bluetooth and Wi-Fi products may also need WPC equipment-type approval [VERIFY with an Indian compliance specialist].
- (Apparel, Home) **BIS Quality Control Orders** require a BIS licence and ISI mark for covered products, foreign makers included. Footwear QCO from Aug 1, 2024 (pre-existing stock sellable until July 31, 2026). [UL](https://www.ul.com/news/textiles-apparel-and-footwear-india-issues-new-quality-control-order-footwear) India is rolling QCOs back after an August 2025 review: the polyester fibre and yarn QCO was withdrawn Nov 12, 2025, and six chemical QCOs in Oct 2025; QCOs had grown to 187 orders covering 769 products. [Swarajya, Nov 2025](https://swarajyamag.com/amp/story/news-brief/india-reconsiders-its-quality-control-push-centre-eases-bis-compliance-burden-with-polyester-qco-withdrawal)
- (All) Legal Metrology: packaged goods need MRP and specific declarations on the label [VERIFY].

**So what:** India needs SKU-level customs modelling and a certification check. Classification, origin, notifications, IGST and BIS/FSSAI/CDSCO approvals can change the economics or block entry altogether.

## 6. Cultural fit risks
- Western-only casting and styling.
- Ignoring festive calendar (Diwali, wedding season, regional festivals).
- Pricing set by converting USD.
- Messaging that sounds preachy about values to a market that has long-standing traditions around natural fibers and craft.

## 7. Category notes
- (Apparel) India has deep textile heritage and is a leading organic cotton producer [VERIFY]. -> An "organic cotton" claim alone is weak in India; craft, fit and design have to carry it.
- (Beauty) Skincare: ingredient-led D2C brands grew fast; Ayurveda-inspired positioning is crowded.
- (Food, Restaurants) Strong local palates, vegetarian demand, price sensitivity.
